I fell asleep on the HSR, missed my transfer, and woke up in Liyang—a town I'd never heard of and definitely wasn't planning to stop at. Checked into a hotel, which took a while because the staff had never processed a foreign passport before.

Instead of rushing straight back to Shanghai the next morning, I decided to actually stop in Hangzhou on the way back. I started with a bowl of noodles in crab broth for lunch, then spent the afternoon walking along West Lake—past dried winter lotus stalks, wooden boats gliding through narrow canals, and tall plane trees lining the misty causeway.

Then the sun started going down and I stopped walking. Pink and purple light spilled across the still water behind the weeping willows and distant mountains—I had seen a lot of places on this trip, but West Lake at dusk hit differently. One of those quiet scenes that makes you feel like you stumbled into something you weren't supposed to see.

Caught the train back to Shanghai after. Genuinely glad I missed my transfer.
