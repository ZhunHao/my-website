Technically a layover on the way to Shanghai. With a day to spare in Macao, I landed at night, rode the monorail past the glowing gold figure-eight Ferris wheel at Studio City, and crossed the border into Zhuhai to sleep.

I woke up in Zhuhai to that golden-hour light and headed down to Lover's Road to cycle along the coast for a while. Riding past the white lighthouse and the twin shells of the Zhuhai Grand Theatre with the sea breeze coming off the water—stumbling into a morning like that by accident made the border crossing worth it.

Around noon, I crossed back into Macao and squeezed in a few hours before my flight. I walked up Calçada da Guia—the narrow colonial streets and bamboo-wrapped facades up there have a completely different character from the casino strip, older and quieter, framing the view down toward the Lisboa Hotel. From there, I headed down to Doca dos Pescadores before ending at the Ruins of St. Paul.
