From Don Mueang, I headed straight to the riverfront for dinner by the Chao Phraya, then walked past the hanging roots of banyan trees in a dark alley before spotting Wat Arun glowing deep electric purple down a narrow side street. After two quiet days on the Jungle Railway, diving straight into the neon canopy of Yaowarat and Nana at night hit like a full sensory overload.
