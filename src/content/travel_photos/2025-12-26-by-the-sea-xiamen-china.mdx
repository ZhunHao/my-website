Next morning I left from Gulangyu harbour back to the mainland side and joined a boat tour out into the strait toward Kinmen. It's a strange thing to actually see it from the water, just a few kilometres away, with the slogans still painted on the hillside. The sunset on the way back was one of the best of the whole trip.
