Christmas night, with a 15-hour layover in Nanjing on the way from Shanghai to Xiamen. I dropped my bags and immediately decided that hiking Purple Mountain at midnight—right past a sign warning against mountaineering in rain, fog, ice, and snow—was the right move.

I made it to the top and genuinely thought I was going to die from the cold. Hiking in subzero Nanjing at 2am was not my finest planning. After catching first light and passing a lone white cat sitting on the path on my way down, I found somewhere warm for breakfast and then took the bus back up like a normal person to see the Purple Mountain Observatory domes under a clear blue sky.

Came back down into the city and walked through the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum grounds, where fallen autumn leaves rested in glass-still puddles and quiet ponds reflecting the branches overhead. I went to the Music Stage too, though the pictures didn't turn out great, so I am skipping those—the walk itself was worth it though.

My last stop was Librairie Avant-Garde, a bookshop built inside a former underground car park. Between the large black cross on the glowing wall, the high ceilings, the rows of shelves, and an installation of hundreds of handwritten notes hanging from the ceiling, the whole space looked incredible.

Then I rushed to the airport and heard my name called over the broadcast at the gate—first time that has ever happened to me. Last person to board, a few minutes to spare. Made it to Xiamen.
