I headed back to Bintan after a year and a half for another round of stargazing with friends. Once night fell, we hauled the telescope down to the sand and spent the entire night looking up. Trading a full night of sleep to watch meteor showers streak across the Milky Way felt completely worth it.

Running on zero hours of sleep, I went straight for the trails and hung out with the local cats—a black-and-white cat sitting right on the hiking path that tolerated a slightly grumpy close-up selfie, and a tabby peeking out from under our table at dinner. Back at the resort, a pair of fluffy rabbits had their own sandy enclosure.
