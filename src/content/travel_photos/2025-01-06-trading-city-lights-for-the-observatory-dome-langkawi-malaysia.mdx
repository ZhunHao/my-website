I left the city glare behind for a few days of quiet in Langkawi. The energy shifts the moment you trade glass skyscrapers for ancient limestone ridges and open water.

During the day, I took the SkyCab cable car up Gunung Machinchang. From the top, smaller islands scatter across the hazy blue Andaman Sea, while macaques sit perched on the grey limestone cliffs watching everyone pass by. By evening, the pace slowed right down along the beach under soft purple and orange clouds reflecting off the tide.

The real highlight came after dark with a night visit to the Langkawi National Observatory. Far from urban light pollution, the sky above the forest opened up completely. Watching the white observatory dome roll back and peering through the massive telescope at a sky packed with stars offered a quiet, unmistakable reminder of how big the world actually feels once the city lights fade.
