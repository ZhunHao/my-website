I landed in Xiamen and took the ferry straight across to Gulangyu. Walking past the old shophouses, banyan trees, and quiet lamplit alleys at night, the whole pace of the island immediately reminded me of Taiwan.

The next day, I took the ferry back from Gulangyu harbour to the mainland side and joined a boat tour out into the strait toward Kinmen. Seeing Kinmen from the water feels surreal—just a few kilometres away, with giant red slogans still painted across the rocky hillside. On the ride back, the sun sank into the strait in one of the best sunsets of the trip.
